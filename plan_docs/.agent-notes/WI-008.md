## Approved policies and scope

- Append-only base-price history: no editing of rows or deletion of future entries in this slice.
- Keep Europe/Dublin as the server business-day boundary, using the existing testable clock.
- Preserve `Product.BasePriceOn(DateOnly)`: latest Effective From on or before the supplied date;
  null before the first entry. Derive ranges from consecutive Effective From dates.
- S3's 1 September–31 October interval applies only without an intervening entry. Existing
  entries are preserved and limit the retroactive entry's interval.
- Keep the exact required duplicate-date wording, including "edit it instead", despite editing
  being unavailable in this approved slice. This is a known wording limitation.
- Orders and tablet snapshots do not exist in this slice. Direct captured-order assertions follow
  their introduction; this work does not add those models solely to support a history assertion.
