## Findings

- Criterion 1 already holds: the snapshot carries the base price in effect on the build date plus every later one
  (`TabletSnapshotBuilder.ReadCatalogueAsync`; `TabletSnapshotApiTests.Should_IncludeTodaysAndFutureBasePrices_When_PricesAreDated`).
- The tablet reads prices with SQL "newest `effective_from <= $today`" (`SnapshotStore.ReadProductIndex(DateOnly today)` in
  `ProductSearchIndex.cs`; `SnapshotStore.Orders.cs ReadOrderableProduct(id, today)`).
- `today` is the device's local date: `TabletApp.Today()` and `OrderStore.Today()` =
  `DateOnly.FromDateTime(clock.GetLocalNow().DateTime)`. `OrderStore.Today()` also stamps the order's `TakenOn` (unchanged by D7).
- The server already uses Europe/Dublin: `ProductEndpoints.BusinessTimeZone`, `Today(TimeProvider)`.
- `SnapshotManifest.BuildDate` (DateOnly) is the snapshot's build day — the clamp floor for D2.
- Lines keep their price on quantity change (`OrderStore.ChangeQuantity`).
- `OfflineCataloguePage` rebuilds on `Tablet.Resumed`; `TabletSessionMonitor` shows the `clock.CreateTimer` pattern.
- Head office's "rising to" display already exists (`Web/Pages/HeadOffice/Products/Detail.cshtml`) — out of scope.

## Approved decisions

- **D1 Business day (MI-53):** the price day is the date in Europe/Dublin, tablet and server alike, via one shared
  `BusinessCalendar.Today(now)` in `FieldSales.Pricing`; `ProductEndpoints.Today` delegates to it. Call and order dates unchanged.
- **D2 Clock:** price day = max(Dublin date by the tablet clock, snapshot `BuildDate`). A clock set forward is trusted.
- **D3 Where:** every tablet price read — the product index (Order entry, stock check catalogue) and adding a line. The clamp lives
  in the snapshot store.
- **D4 Midnight:** `PriceDayWatch` (Tablet.Core, `TimeProvider` timer each minute) raises `TabletApp.PriceDayChanged`; open pages
  refresh as on resume, keeping drafts.
- **D5 Captured lines:** a line keeps its price; a quantity change never re-prices; new lines after midnight get the new price;
  the server stores lines as received.
- **D6 Engine input:** the tablet keeps its "newest on or before the day" read, fed by the price day; engine wiring into lines is
  WI-064/WI-065 (inferred).
- **D7 Out of scope:** tiers/breaks in the snapshot (T-6.4.2), price display (T-6.4.3), head office editing (T-1.3.1), order and
  call dates, clock detection at Sync.
- **D8 Finish:** push, PR, merge after CI (merge commit, branch kept), update local main.
