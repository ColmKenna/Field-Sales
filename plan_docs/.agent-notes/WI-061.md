## Findings

- The engine already has `PriceListTier(Name, Prices)` in `src/FieldSales.Pricing/PricingInputs.cs`; `TierCandidates.For` gives a
  listed product its price and an unlisted one nothing (list price stands). No engine change is needed.
- `PriceResolver` ranks a tie tier → break → list, so a fixed price equal to list price shows as the tier.
- Products have no archived state, so the card's "product later archived" edge can't arise (as in WI-060).
- Stored tiers are read only by `PriceTierEndpoints` (`ToEngine`) and the website's tier pages; nothing on the tablet yet.

## Approved decisions

- **D1 Percentage → price list:** fixed overrides carry over as listed prices; percentage overrides are removed, named in the
  confirm step ("These 2 percentage overrides will be removed: Hand Cream 75ml, …"); the default percentage is cleared.
- **D2 Confirm step:** "Change to price list…" / "Change to percentage…" opens a confirmation stating what stops applying
  ("Tier B's 8% off base will no longer apply. Products not listed will revert to base price." plus D1's list; back to percentage:
  "Products not listed will get 8% off base."), with Confirm and Cancel. Nothing changes until Confirm.
- **D3 Above-list warning:** a fixed price saves straight away; its row shows "This is above list price and will never apply"
  whenever it is above today's list price (Europe/Dublin business day), on percentage-tier fixed overrides and price-list entries
  alike. No warning when equal to list (it ties, the tier shows) or when the product has no list price today (inferred). Worked out
  on read, never stored.
- **D4 Price list → percentage:** head office enters the new default percentage on the confirm step; listed prices stay as fixed
  overrides.
- **D5 Storage (inferred):** a `Shape` column on `PriceTiers` (Percentage / PriceList); `PercentOff` nullable with a check
  (a percentage only, and always, on percentage tiers); price-list entries reuse `PriceTierOverrides` rows with a fixed price; the
  domain refuses a percentage entry on a price list. One additive migration; existing rows become Percentage. No stored prices.
- **D6 API (inferred):** create takes a shape (none sent = percentage, so WI-060's request still works); a price list refuses a
  percentage ("A price-list tier has no default percentage." / "Enter a fixed price; a price list holds fixed prices only."); a
  new shape-change endpoint; detail adds the shape and a per-row above-list flag; the list adds the shape. Access and last-save-wins
  unchanged.
- **D7 Pages (inferred):** create form with a Percentage / Price list choice (percentage needed only for Percentage); list reads
  "Suncare Deal 2027 · price list · 20 products" beside "Tier B · 8% off base · 2 overrides"; a price-list detail says "Listed
  prices", offers a fixed price only, and "No listed prices yet. Every product is at base price."
- **D8 Out of scope:** drift indicators (E19/WI-157), tier assignment and customer counts (T-6.2.1), category overrides (MI-47),
  deleting tiers.
- **D9 Finish:** as WI-060 if the developer says so at the time: push, PR, merge after CI (merge commit, branch kept), update
  local main.
