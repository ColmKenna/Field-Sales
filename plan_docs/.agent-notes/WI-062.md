## Findings

- The engine already has `QuantityBreak(FromQuantity, Price)` on `PricedProduct.Breaks` and `BreakCandidate.For` (every break at
  or below the quantity is reached; the lowest-priced applies; equal prices go to the lower threshold). Label "Break: 10+" /
  "Break: 10 kg+". `EngineInputs.ToPricedProduct` passes no breaks yet ("Quantity breaks reach products with T-6.3.1").
- `QuantityRules` (FieldSales.Quantities) validates order quantities: Each whole and at least 1; measures at least the minimum and
  a multiple of the step ("Enter multiples of 0.5 kg").
- Product endpoints live in `src/FieldSales.Api/Catalogue/ProductEndpoints.cs` (policy `HeadOfficeCatalogue`); the product page
  is `src/FieldSales.Web/Pages/HeadOffice/Products/Detail.cshtml` with sections (Base price, Unit, …) and handlers per form.
- Quantities are stored at `Quantity.Precision`/`Quantity.Scale` (18, 6).

## Decisions (assumed, developer pre-approved)

- **D1 Storage:** a new table `ProductQuantityBreaks` (ProductId, FromQuantity decimal(18,6), Price decimal(18,2)), key
  (ProductId, FromQuantity), checks FromQuantity > 0 and Price > 0, foreign key to the product. Breaks belong to the product, not
  a tier. Not dated: they apply from when saved (inferred).
- **D2 Editing (H22.1):** a product's breaks are saved as a set: `PUT /catalogue/products/{id}/quantity-breaks` with the whole
  list replaces them (an empty list removes all). On the product page, a "Quantity breaks" section after Base price shows the base
  price above the breaks ("Base price €2.20 each"), the breaks sorted by quantity, each editable with a Remove button, one blank
  row to add, and Save.
- **D3 Rules:** a threshold must be an orderable quantity of the product (whole for Each; a multiple of the step for measures,
  "Enter multiples of 0.5 kg") and above the smallest orderable quantity (1 for Each, the minimum for measures): "A break starts
  above the smallest quantity you can order (1)." Prices more than €0.00, to the cent ("Enter a price more than €0.00, to the
  cent."). Two breaks at the same quantity are refused ("Each break needs its own quantity.").
- **D4 Warnings (H22.2):** saved anyway; worked out on reading, never stored.
  - "This break is higher than the one below it and will never apply." when its price is above the lowest-priced break at a
    smaller quantity (the engine applies the lowest reached break).
  - "This break is above the base price and will never apply." when above today's base price (inferred, as WI-061 D3).
  - Equal prices: no warning (inferred).
- **D5 Unit changes:** a unit, step or minimum change that would leave a break off the new rules is refused, naming it:
  "Change or remove the quantity breaks first: from 12.5 kg doesn't fit." (inferred; keeps criterion 5 true).
- **D6 Engine:** `ToPricedProduct` passes the breaks; callers load them.
- **D7 Access:** the existing `HeadOfficeCatalogue` policy.
- **D8 Out of scope:** breaks in the snapshot and on the tablet (T-6.4.2/WI-064), break prompts (T-6.5.1), breaks per tier.
- **D9 Finish:** push, PR, merge after CI (merge commit, branch kept), update local main (pre-approved).
