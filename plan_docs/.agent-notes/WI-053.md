"Ordered since" — **owner's ruling at the checkpoint (2026-11-05): "ordered since is any order since the date".** Any order at the
Location dated on or after the Call's date counts, whatever its status or route; no status filter (today's statuses are only Pending
and Accepted, so a status added later still counts).

"Ordered since" routes:
- Server: every `ReceivedOrders` row at the Location with `TakenOn >= CalledOn`, any status (no filter), lines from the received body.
  `TabletWorkReceiver` is the only writer of received orders, so this is every order route the system has today (rep tablets).
  Customer online and chain orders don't exist yet; they would join this query.
- Tablet: its own orders at the Location (unsent work, any state incl. Needs Attention, and the sent table) with `TakenOn >= CalledOn`,
  except the order on screen (its lines show ADDED instead).
- Not knowable: orders still unsynced on another tablet.

Size: at the D3 target scale (500 Locations), one row per carried product of each Location's latest Call: ~5–30 per Location →
2,500–15,000 rows of ~130 bytes JSON (0.3–2 MB plain, far less gzipped), against the 120,000 history lines the target profile already
carries within the 90 s refresh budget.

## Approved decisions

- **D1 Snapshot 1.4** (minor; older apps ignore): one family `LastCallLowLines(LocationId, CallId, CalledOn, ProductId)`: each
  Location's most recent Call (any channel, by anyone, as WI-049's last stock check), its Low entries for included products, minus
  products on any received order at that Location taken on or after the Call's date. Tablet snapshot SQLite schema 3 → 4 with an
  upgrade step, as WI-049.
- **D2 "Ordered since":** server = every received order (Pending or Accepted); today only rep tablet orders, customer online / chain
  join the same query when they exist. Tablet also subtracts its own orders at the Location dated on/after the Call (unsent, or sent
  since the snapshot), except the order on screen; a carried product on the order on screen shows ADDED (WI-052 `LowItems`).
- **D3 Most recent Call:** the snapshot's, unless the tablet holds a newer unsynced saved Call at that Location; then that Call's Low
  items carry (after the same subtractions).
- **D4 Wording:** "No stock check with this order." always, then "Still open from your call at Carey's - Tue 15 Sep"; rows
  "marked Low 15 Sep"; count includes carried not added; no age limit; **Record call** link in both cases. "your call" even if another
  rep recorded it.
- **D5 Swap on call (S22):** Record call from the Low tab opens `/locations/{id}/orders/{orderId}/calls/new`. On Save: the order is
  linked to the Call (new `OrderStore.LinkToCall`, In Progress orders with no Call only) and the rep returns to order entry on
  WI-052's call route, whose Low tab shows that Call's Low items. Back (unsaved) returns to the order. An order with no lines yet
  doesn't exist, so the rep returns to a new order opened from that Call (links on its first line).
- **D6 Out of scope:** CAN'T ADD (E8; carried items can be not added only, so S19's Aftersun row can't appear yet), REPLACED (E10),
  customer online / chain orders until they exist.
- **D7 Finish:** push, PR, merge after CI (merge commit, branch kept), update local main.
