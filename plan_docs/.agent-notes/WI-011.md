## Approved decisions (developer, 2026-10-01)

- **D1 (MI-45):** while a Restriction Group is archived, its products are hidden from every rep,
  with or without the permission. This fails closed. Products with no group are unaffected.
- **D1b:** the archive confirmation also says "N products will be hidden from every rep while
  archived" (singular: "1 product will be hidden from every rep while archived").
- **D2:** a product cannot be newly assigned to an archived group. An unchanged existing link is
  kept.
- **D3:** un-archive keeps the existing confirmation step and adds "N permissions will take effect
  again" (singular: "1 permission will take effect again").
- **D4:** the singular S5 wording is "1 permission will be kept but has no effect while archived".
- **D5:** the visibility rule is built now, as one IQueryable filter in the Catalogue that takes the
  rep's permitted group ids. WI-029 supplies the permissions and WI-043 calls the filter.

## Design notes

- The production required usage source is `products` only. WI-029 must register a
  `restriction-permissions` source (labels permission/permissions) and add it to the list's
  required sources. Until then the S5 count is 0 in production, and tests use a fake source, as
  WI-009 did for the specialist count.
- The wording lives in the Restriction Group list definition. The shared confirmation renders it,
  with no special case for Restriction Groups in the markup.
