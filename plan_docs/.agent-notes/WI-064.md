## Findings

- The tablet prices from base prices only: `ProductSearchIndex` (pad and search) and `SnapshotStore.ReadOrderableProduct` (line
  capture) read the base price in effect on the price day (WI-059). `OrderStore.AddLine` captures `Base` / `Base price` with one
  candidate. `QuantityPopover` and `NewOrder.razor` show `ProductSearchResult.Price`.
- `OrderStore.ChangeQuantity` and adding more to a line keep the captured price (D6 of an earlier item; WI-059 D4: "a quantity
  change never re-prices").
- The snapshot contract is at 1.5 (`SnapshotContract.CurrentMinor`); the tablet store is at format 5 and steps formats up one at a
  time. Families are read by `SnapshotDocumentReader` and inserted by `SnapshotStore.Insert`; counts are checked against the manifest.
- The server's `CustomerTierPricing` (WI-063) already gives a Location's customer's tiers; `ProductQuantityBreaks` (WI-062) hold the
  breaks.

## Decisions (assumed, developer pre-approved)

- **D1 Snapshot 1.6** (a minor, additive version): new families `priceTiers` (id, name, shape, percent off), `priceTierTerms`
  (tier, product, percent off or fixed price), `customerTiers` (customer, tier) and `quantityBreaks` (product, from quantity,
  price), with their counts in the manifest. Only tiers held by the customers of the rep's assigned Locations, and only terms and
  breaks for products the snapshot includes. The tablet store moves to format 6 with four tables and reference checks.
- **D2 One engine on the tablet:** a Location's prices come from one read (its customer's tiers, the products' breaks, the price
  day) and `PriceResolver`; the pad, the popover and capture all use it. No second rule.
- **D3 Pad price:** each product's resolved price at its smallest orderable quantity (1 for Each, the minimum for a measure) for
  this Location's customer (inferred, as WI-063's sample).
- **D4 Popover:** the winner at the quantity typed.
- **D5 Capture:** a new line captures the engine's winner at its quantity with every candidate, the winner marked. A list-price
  winner is captured exactly as before (`Base` / `Base price`), so orders at list price look as they did; tier and break winners
  carry the engine's kind and label (`Tier` / "Tier B", `Break` / "Break: 10+").
- **D6 Later quantity changes keep the captured price**, as the developer decided in WI-059 (D4). Not changed here; flagged for
  WI-066 (break prompts), which needs a line to re-price as its quantity crosses a break.
- **D7** Lines captured before this change keep their prices; nothing re-prices them.
- **D8 Out of scope:** source and runner-up display and the provenance sheet (T-6.4.3, WI-065), break prompts (T-6.5.1, WI-066),
  promotions (E18).
- **D9 Finish:** push, PR, merge after CI (merge commit, branch kept), update local main (pre-approved).
