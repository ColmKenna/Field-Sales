## Approved decisions

- **D1 Rounding (MI-48):** unit price (per item or per kg) to the cent, half away from zero, when a percentage is applied; line value =
  rounded unit price × quantity, rounded the same way.
- **D2 Overrides (MI-47):** per product only — percentage tier: default % plus product overrides (a % or a fixed price); price-list
  tier: fixed prices; an unlisted product gets no tier candidate.
- **D3 Ties:** tier, then break, then list; equal tiers by name (ordinal, alphabetical); extra candidates rank after these until E18
  (inferred).
- **D4 Breaks:** every break at or below the quantity is reached; the lowest of them is the one break candidate (a rising break never
  applies). Each and measured units alike.
- **D5 Output:** winner, runner-up tier, every candidate cheapest first (ties per D3), each with kind, label, amount. Labels "List
  price", the tier name, "Break: 10+" / "Break: 10 kg+". Maps onto `OrderLinePrice`. No candidate → no price.
- **D6 Runner-up and summary:** runner-up = best tier price when the winner isn't a tier. Summary e.g. "€11.20 — Tier B (list €12.50)",
  "€12.50 — list price", "€10.08 — Break: 24+ (better than your tier price €11.20)", "per kg" for measured products.
- **D7 Shared module:** `src/FieldSales.Pricing` (no I/O, no deps) referenced by Tablet.Core and Api; tests in
  `tests/Unit/FieldSales.Pricing` (Category=Unit). One implementation, so no separate vectors file.
- **D8 Inputs:** `Resolve(product, tiers, quantity, date, extraCandidates = null)`; the date is the caller's calendar date (MI-53 is
  WI-059's). E18 adds candidates; E19 post-processes the result and adds its working.
- **D9 Out of scope:** tier/break setup (WI-060–062), assigning tiers (WI-063), display (WI-064, 065), prompts (WI-066), promotions
  (E18), allowances (E19), the business time zone (WI-059).
- **D10 Finish:** push, PR, merge after CI (merge commit, branch kept), update local main.
